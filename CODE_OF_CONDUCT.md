# Code of conduct

We expect contributors and maintainers to make participation respectful, safe, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, evidence, and tradeoffs without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Give specific, actionable review feedback.
- Respect privacy, licensing, embargoes, and responsible disclosure.
- Stop behavior when a maintainer says it is unwelcome or disruptive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of private information without permission are not accepted in project spaces or project-related public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to `security@encypher.com`. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, and other material that violates this policy. They may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban a participant based on severity and pattern.

Reports will be handled as privately as the facts allow. A maintainer named in a report will not decide its outcome.
